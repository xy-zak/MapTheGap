shared vocabulary tag overlap of 0.50 or better — shared tags divided by all tags across both — on subject, question and approach. Drawn only where no citation exists, since a citation says strictly more.
position an output of the links, not the year: citations pull hard, vocabulary weakly, every source repels every other. Only who sits beside whom means anything — distance between far-apart groups does not. Vocabulary is drawn thin and faint, so a group joined only by dashes shares language but cites nothing. spread (compact · balanced · distinct) sets how hard the clusters are pulled apart; compact also drops the layout's head start, placing every source by its links alone.
cluster hulls communities found on the links alone, independently of the layout, each named by its most distinctive tag. Select a source and every hull covering it turns solid and names itself — one sitting where two strands overlap names both. They are the cross-check: a hull that comes out long and tangled means the maths and the physics disagree, so do not believe that grouping.
